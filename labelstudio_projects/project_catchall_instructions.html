Catchall word review

Review one Cyrillic word form at a time and approve its final Zamanalif spelling.

What To Do

  1. Read the original Cyrillic word.
  2. If the word can be either native or Russian depending on sentence context, check Homonym and submit.
  3. Otherwise, check and, if needed, edit the suggested Zamanalif spelling.
  4. Submit, or skip the task when you cannot decide reliably.

Conversion Focus

Check the proposed spelling, especially these common conditional cases:

  • в: usually w in native/Tatar words and v in Russian or Russian-international words. Examples: вакыт → waqıt; совет → sovet.
  • к: often q in back-vowel native words, k in front-vowel words and many loanwords. Examples: халык → xalıq; кеше → keşe; комитет → komitet.
  • г: often ğ in back-vowel native words, g in front-vowel words and many loanwords. Examples: туган → tuğan; гөл → göl; грант → grant.
  • е: may be ye, yı, or e depending on position and word type. Examples: егет → yeget; ел → yıl; проект → proyekt.
  • я: may be ya, yä, a, or ä depending on context. Examples: яңа → yaña; сәяхәт → säyäxät; әкият → äkiät.
  • ю: may be yu, yü, or another contextual form. Examples: юл → yul; юкә → yükä; бию → biü.
  • ц: may be s or ts, mostly in loanwords. Examples: цирк → sirk; позиция → pozitsiä.
  • у / ү: may interact with nearby vowels or produce a w-like glide. Examples: уку → uqu; күрү → kürü; саклау → saqlaw.

Editing Rules

  • Write one complete final Zamanalif word form on each line.
  • Do not enter DSL syntax, explanations, meanings, lemmas, or alternatives.
  • Preserve Zamanalif letters exactly: ä ö ü ñ ı ğ ş ç and their uppercase forms.
  • Use the apostrophe ʼ when the selected spelling requires one.
  • Skip malformed or genuinely uncertain items.
