RL FINAL KA

Review one Cyrillic word form at a time and approve its final Zamanalif spelling.

What To Do

  1. Read the original Cyrillic word.
  2. If the word can be either native or Russian depending on sentence context, check Homonym and submit.
  3. Otherwise, check and, if needed, edit the suggested Zamanalif spelling.
  4. Submit, or skip the task when you cannot decide reliably.

Conversion Focus

The alternatives below are attested conventions. Enter the complete word, not DSL syntax.

  • RL_FINAL_KA: Decide whether к belongs to the Russian stem or starts a Tatar suffix. Examples: булавка → bulavqa / bulavka; кубка → kubqa / kubka.

Editing Rules

  • Write one complete final Zamanalif word form on each line.
  • Replace an invalid variant line with -. Keep the line order and at least one word; rejecting alternatives must leave exactly one word.
  • Do not enter DSL syntax, explanations, meanings, lemmas, or alternatives.
  • Preserve Zamanalif letters exactly: ä ö ü ñ ı ğ ş ç and their uppercase forms.
  • Use the apostrophe ʼ when the selected spelling requires one.
  • Skip malformed or genuinely uncertain items.
