Complex multi-rule words

Review one Cyrillic word form at a time and approve its final Zamanalif spelling.

What To Do

  1. Read the original Cyrillic word.
  2. If the word can be either native or Russian depending on sentence context, check Homonym and submit.
  3. Otherwise, check and, if needed, edit the suggested Zamanalif spelling.
  4. Submit, or skip the task when you cannot decide reliably.

Conversion Focus

The alternatives below are attested conventions. Enter the complete word, not DSL syntax.

  • MONTH_NAME: Choose ordinary transliteration or the special month-name spelling. Examples: гыйнвар → ğıynwar / ğinwar; ноябрь → noyabr / noyäbr.
  • RUS_SIGN: Choose whether a Russian soft or hard sign is omitted or represented by ʼ. Examples: роль → rol / rolʼ; компьютер → kompyuter / kompʼyuter.
  • RL_Y: Choose whether Russian-loan ы is represented as short ı or long ıy. Examples: сыр → sır / sıyr; посылка → posılka / posıylka.
  • RUS_JOTATION: Choose whether Russian я/ю uses a y glide, an apostrophe, or neither. Examples: бюро → byuro / bʼuro / buro.
  • RL_FINAL_KA: Decide whether к belongs to the Russian stem or starts a Tatar suffix. Examples: булавка → bulavqa / bulavka; кубка → kubqa / kubka.
  • IYA: Choose whether Cyrillic ия is written compactly or with an explicit y glide. Examples: орфография → orfografiä / orfografiyä; әдәбият → ädäbiat / ädäbiyat.
  • E_GLIDE: Choose whether е is plain e or has an explicit y glide. Examples: проект → proekt / proyekt.
  • NATIVE_UW: Choose whether native у/ү before another vowel has an explicit w glide. Examples: буа → bua / buwa; китүе → kitüe / kitüwe.

Editing Rules

  • Write only one complete final Zamanalif word form.
  • Do not enter DSL syntax, explanations, meanings, lemmas, or alternatives.
  • Preserve Zamanalif letters exactly: ä ö ü ñ ı ğ ş ç and their uppercase forms.
  • Use the apostrophe ʼ when the selected spelling requires one.
  • Skip malformed or genuinely uncertain items.
